About me

My name is Ayobami and I am a creative specializing in Film Production and Motion Graphics, I work on delivering high quality marketing assets towards brand and creative advertising. With a BA in Media and Film from the University of Windsor, my professional journey commenced as an In-House VFX Editor at MPC, a leading VFX agency. There, I managed data traffic within the VFX production pipeline, meticulously organizing visual assets and optimizing workflow efficiency.

Working as a freelance Media Producer further honed my skills, allowing me to collaborate with various brands to develop and execute diverse digital content strategies. These projects included creating engaging multimedia pieces, translations, banners, and editorial assets.
